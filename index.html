Automated video pipeline

{{YouTube TheRandomBox}}

{{YouTube TheRandomBox}} is a scheduled automation that assembles short vertical videos from a rotating set of source material and publishes them to a single YouTube channel. It runs unattended on a fixed schedule, with no public-facing app or account system of its own.

What it does

On each run, the pipeline selects source content, builds a short video with narration, captions, or background music as appropriate, and uploads the result directly to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. Nothing is published anywhere else, and no other accounts are involved.

Who operates this

This project is operated and maintained by a single developer for a personal YouTube channel. It is not a multi-tenant service, and it does not offer accounts, logins, or access to other users' data.
